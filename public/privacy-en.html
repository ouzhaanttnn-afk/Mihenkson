Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the MIHENKAYNAK mobile game handles local saves and Google AdMob advertising data.

Last updated: September 4, 2026 · Developer: Nostoscomp

In short: Your game progress stays on your device and is not sent to a developer-owned server. Optional rewarded ads and week-opening interstitial ads are provided by Google AdMob, whose Mobile Ads SDK may process the data described below for advertising, safety, and performance.

1. Local game data

Your shop name, selected character, progress, cash, stock, and preferences are stored only in local storage on your device. MIHENKAYNAK has no account or developer-operated cloud-save server. Deleting the app or clearing its data may erase this save.

The app's own code does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location and does not use a separate developer analytics or crash-reporting service.

2. Google AdMob advertising

The app has no banner ads. It may show:

  • Rewarded ads after you deliberately choose a clearly labelled in-game advertising benefit. The reward is granted only after completion.
  • Interstitial ads when a new in-game week opens; these grant no reward.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process IP address and approximate location derived from it, device or advertising identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, app interactions, performance data, and non-identifying crash or diagnostic data. Processing may support ad delivery and measurement, fraud prevention, security, analytics, and performance. This data is sent to Google's systems.

See Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure and Privacy Policy for details.

3. ATT and consent choices

On iOS, App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission is requested before an advertising identifier can be used to track activity across companies' apps and websites. If you decline, the app remains usable and ads may be non-personalised or limited.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and other consent-required regions, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) may show a consent form when required. Declining consent does not block the game's core features. When Google requires a privacy-options entry point, it is available in Settings.

4. Sharing and retention

The developer cannot access or sell your local save. Retention and sharing of AdMob data are governed by Google's policies and your regional advertising and consent choices.

5. Children's privacy

This general-audience simulation game does not ask for age, legal name, email, or account details. A parent or guardian may contact us with any privacy concern.

6. Changes and contact

If cloud save, accounts, another ad provider, or developer analytics are added, this policy and the store privacy disclosures will be updated. For privacy questions or requests, email nostoscomp@gmail.com.
