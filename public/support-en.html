Player support

How can we help?

Contact the developer directly for bug reports, ad/reward problems, privacy questions, or accessibility feedback.

Email support

Include your device model, iOS or Android version, the game screen where the problem appeared, and a screenshot if possible. Do not send passwords or payment details; MIHENKAYNAK will never ask for them.

nostoscomp@gmail.com

Where is my save stored?

Progress is saved automatically on your device. This release does not connect to an App Store, Google Play, or other cloud account. Deleting the app or clearing its data may erase your save.

I completed an ad but did not receive the reward

Check your network connection and reopen the game. If the issue continues, include the ad type, approximate time, and device in your email. The game cannot grant a reward when an ad is closed early or the provider does not confirm completion.

Privacy or advertising choices

Read the Privacy Policy for data processing, ATT, and Google AdMob details. You may also send privacy requests to the support address.
